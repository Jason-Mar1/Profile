About Me

I am currently pursuing my PhD at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Soochow University. My research lies in computer vision and multimodal learning, focusing on reliable object and crowd counting in dense scenes, and on building efficient, generalizable visual understanding systems with multimodal large language models (MLLM) and linear attention mechanisms.

I am particularly interested in combining visual perception tasks with the reasoning capability of large models. Through algorithm design and system-level optimization, I aim to substantially reduce computational cost while preserving accuracy. Collaborations and discussions are warmly welcomed.

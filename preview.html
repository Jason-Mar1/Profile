PhD Student · School of Computer Science and Technology · Soochow University

I am a PhD student at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Soochow University. My research centers on object & crowd counting, multimodal learning, multimodal large language models (MLLMs), and linear attention. I work toward robust and efficient vision-language systems that can perceive, count, and reason about complex scenes, with several published papers and open-source projects in this area.
